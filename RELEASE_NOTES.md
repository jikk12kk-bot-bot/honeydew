# Honeydew 0.0.13: numbers on every slider, bigger mixer levels

## Values
- The drum strips' sliders show their number again on every page: Pan and Level on `Mixer` and Hold on `Seq`. Where a row is too short to fit the number over the slider, the slider moves down a little and the number sits above it (under the `Hold` label).
- A send's level in the rack (`SENDS`) shows its number above the slider.

## Mixer
- The level in dB is bigger (12 pt, or 10 pt for a long number in a narrow strip) and sits half way between the bottom of the fader and the pan knob.
